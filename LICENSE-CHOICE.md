# License choice

## Option 1 · All rights reserved

People may read the public repository, but they do not receive permission to copy, redistribute, modify or use its
documentation and images. This gives the team the most control but makes legitimate educational sharing harder.

## Option 2 · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

People may share the documentation and images if they credit the Neerdhara team, do not use them commercially and
do not distribute changed versions. The private source code is not covered because it is not in this repository.

## Recommendation

Use **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0** for the public documentation and images. It supports judging, educational sharing and
portfolio visibility while preventing commercial reuse and modified copies. Until the team confirms that choice,
the repository remains all rights reserved.
