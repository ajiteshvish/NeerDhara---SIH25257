# How it works

## Normal maintenance flow

1. The node samples tank level and safety signals.
2. A regular reading is sent every five minutes, or sooner when a configured limit is crossed.
3. The rules engine opens an alert with a visible severity and status.
4. The household receives simple guidance and can mark a cleaning attempt or ask for help.
5. The Gram Panchayat reviews the alert and assigns a field operator.
6. The operator starts the task, follows a checklist and adds before/after photos.
7. After submission, the alert waits for normal sensor readings.
8. Confirmed recovery closes the alert; unresolved conditions can be escalated.

The Android app keeps useful cached information and queues supported field actions while connectivity is poor.

## Flood flow

Flood wetness changes the unit state and contributes to a village flood event. Flood Watch groups affected units,
shows the flood zone and lets authorised staff queue a household warning. The treatment path remains gravity-fed;
the monitoring system supports the response but is not a gate that water must pass through.

## Evidence and oversight

Officials see scoped dashboards, trends, village rankings, task response, flood history and report exports. Changes
to users, devices and alert rules are recorded in the audit history.
