# Users and screens

## Roles

- **Household:** understands its own unit, follows simple guidance and reports problems.
- **Field operator:** receives work, records evidence and installs units.
- **Panchayat:** manages its village alerts, approvals and flood response.
- **Block official:** monitors villages, maintenance performance and reports in one block.
- **District official:** compares blocks and villages across the district.
- **Admin:** manages geography, users, devices, rules and system health.

Access is restricted by both role and assigned area.
