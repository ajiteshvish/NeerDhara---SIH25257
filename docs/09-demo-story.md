# Seven-minute demo story

## 1 · See the block

Start on the block dashboard. Show three seeded villages, live unit states, open alerts, a level trend and ranking.

## 2 · Catch risk early

Raise a seeded household unit to warning level. On the household app, point out the yellow state and tap **I need
help** after the guided first response.

## 3 · Assign local help

Open the alert as the Rampur panchayat and assign Ramesh Kumar. The same alert is visible from the official web
queue as a fallback.

## 4 · Fix with evidence

On the operator app, start the task, complete the checklist, add before/after photos and submit. Explain the
**waiting for sensor** state. Return the reading to normal and show automatic closure.

## 5 · Respond to flood

Trigger the Rampur flood state. Show Flood Watch on the web, then the panchayat's full-screen village warning and
the queued household alert. Be explicit that voice delivery is simulated in this prototype.

## 6 · Install and approve

Walk through the operator installation wizard, including location, photos and sensor test. Approve the pending unit
from the panchayat app.

## 7 · Report and close

Preview a monthly report and export it. Finish with the ESP32 node and the key safety point: water still moves by
gravity if electronics or connectivity fail.
